= START
# PotentialCrates: dig for records
Tap a crate or tap ROLL. PotentialCrates finds records on Discogs and streams one into your MPC track, ready to sample.

- Load it as a plugin instrument (a plugin program). It needs a network connection.
- CRATES page: eight one-tap digs. Each one searches and plays the first result.
- DIG page: SEARCH digs with your filters, RESULTS lists 8 a page, tap one to play it.
- If a dig finds nothing, it digs again with new picks, up to 3 times.

The full PotentialSuite manual: github.com/PotentialBreakfast/potential-plugins

= CRATES
# The eight crates
- DRUM BREAKS: Funk, Soul, Jazz-Funk, Boogaloo and Afrobeat, 60s and 70s.
- LIBRARY: library records across Stage & Screen, Jazz and Electronic, 60s to 80s.
- SOUNDTRACKS: Soundtrack and Score, 60s to 80s.
- JAZZ-FUNK: Jazz-Funk, Soul-Jazz and Fusion.
- PSYCH: Psychedelic Rock, Krautrock and psychedelic Soul.
- BOSSA & SAMBA: Bossanova, Samba, MPB and Bossa Nova jazz.
- DISCO & BOOGIE: Disco and Boogie, 70s and 80s.
- DUB & REGGAE: Dub, Roots Reggae and Rocksteady.

= DIG
# DIG and FILTERS pages
- SEARCH digs Discogs with your filters. Leave them all on ANY for anything at all.
- FILTERS: genre, style, decade, region and country with arrows. CLEAR FILTERS sets them all back to ANY.
- ROLL (FILTERS and CRATES pages) picks the filters for you, searches and plays the result.
- MILD stays in the genre you are in and picks a style of it, 60s or 70s. MEDIUM picks a digger's genre, 50s to 90s. WILD picks any genre, style and decade, and sometimes a country.
- SOURCE: CHANNEL digs random uploads from the YouTube channels you list in channels.txt.
- PLAY, STOP, BACK 15 and FWD 15 are the transport. NEXT skips to the next result.
- AUTO NEXT moves on when a track ends or will not play.

= SAMPLING
# Sampling what you find
- TRANSPORT SYNC: a track loads and waits, silent, until you press Play on the MPC, and the MPC's Stop pauses it. This is for resampling in time.
- Record the track's output with the MPC's sampler or looper, then chop it.
- Save what you record into your MELODIC LIBRARY folder for PotentialWax, or your DRUM BREAKS LIBRARY folder for PotentialBreaks, then RESCAN there.
- The status line says why a track will not play: private video, removed, region blocked, rate limited or no network.

Check that you have the right to use what you sample before you release it.

= SUITE
# With the other plugins
- Crates finds it, PotentialWax flips melodies, PotentialBreaks chops drums.
- Dusty Loop chain on a sampled loop: Bleed > Patina > Grit, the same preset name in each.
- Break Bus chain on sampled drums: Punch > Grit > Bleed > Patina.
- PotentialNoise gives a balanced rough mix once the beat is built.

= HELP
# Good to know
- Your project remembers the filters, source, ROLL setting, SYNC, AUTO NEXT and volume.
- channels.txt in the plugin's folder lists the YouTube channels for SOURCE: CHANNEL, one per line.
- Streaming needs the MPC online. If nothing plays, check the network first, then the status line.
- Q-Links: AUTO NEXT and SYNC on DIG, VOLUME and the ROLL setting on CRATES.
- Looks: Print Plate, Night Rack or Colour Block, on the SETTINGS page.
